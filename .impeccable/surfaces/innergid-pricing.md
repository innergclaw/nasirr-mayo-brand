# INNERG ID pricing and briefing access

## Direction contract

- THESIS: Give people two clear ways to pay without hiding the better long-term value of membership.
- OWN-WORLD: Preserve the established INNERG cream, ink, acid-green, compact type, and verified-ID visual language.
- STORY: Show the preview, explain membership value, present monthly and annual access, then offer the single briefing as a clean alternative.
- FIRST VIEWPORT: On the briefing page, show the title, 27-minute scope, $19 one-time price, no-membership requirement, and cover image.
- FORM: Use an editorial pricing comparison, one dominant membership panel, a full-width one-time purchase strip, and a protected watch surface. Avoid a generic card grid.
- MODE: Persuade.

## Product rules

- New monthly membership is $15.
- Twelve months cost $150 once and do not auto-renew.
- Existing $10 monthly founding memberships keep their original rate.
- The standalone premium briefing is $19 once.
- Active paid members and active seven-day trial members receive the briefing without another charge.
- Financial content must remain educational and must not promise results.
