# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

INNERG INTEL serves learners, builders, creators, entrepreneurs, new investors, and curious people who want more than disconnected information. New visitors need a clear explanation of the system before they decide whether to read, join, or claim a trial.

## Product Purpose

INNERG INTEL is a learning and ownership system founded by Nasirr G. Mayo. It helps people understand the systems that affect their lives, examine the ideas shaping their decisions, and use what they learn to build something useful.

Success means a person can find context, strengthen independent thought, and move from consumption toward useful action and ownership.

## Positioning

INNERG INTEL connects market education, technology, artificial intelligence, history, culture, books, creative work, digital ownership, and community learning through one framework and one verified INNERG ID.

Different interests are entry points into the same system. The INNERG ID connects identity, access, purchases, resources, and community benefits without requiring a separate account for every part of the ecosystem.

## Operating Context

People can enter through public articles, videos, books, market charts, lessons, and community discussion. Paid access adds the full media library, member research, market insights, community tools, and active benefits.

Five one-time seven-day trials open each Sunday. A verified claim starts the trial. Each account and verified email can claim once.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- GitHub Pages is the public web destination.
- Supabase handles verified accounts, the INNERG ID record, and time-limited member access.
- Market content is educational. It must show risk and must not promise returns or present random picks as a system.
- Public education and paid member access must remain clear and separate.
- An INNERG ID can remain attached to a person when time-limited or paid access changes.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the exact public name `INNERG INTEL`.
- Preserve the tagline `learn the system. recode the mind. build the future.`
- Credit Nasirr G. Mayo as founder.
- Use a grounded, culturally aware, direct teaching voice.
- Use lowercase for conversational public education when it matches the established page.
- Preserve the existing INNERG member badge and the established public routes.

## Product Principles

1. Explain the system before asking for action.
2. Strengthen independent thought instead of telling people what to think.
3. Connect learning to useful work and ownership.
4. Keep public education useful without exposing paid member material.
5. Preserve one identity as access changes across the ecosystem.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public pages must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, semantic structure, and responsive layouts for desktop and mobile visitors.
